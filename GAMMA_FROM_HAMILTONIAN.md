# Deriving the factorized γ<1 scattering model from a toy Hamiltonian

*Companion to `lean/mathlib/QIQTH/CollisionalGamma.lean`. Closes (for a toy model)
the one isolated physical input flagged by the GPT-5.5-pro review: "derive a
stable, factorized, branch-dependent scattering model with uniform per-collision
distinguishability γ<1 from a concrete Hamiltonian."*

## 1. What the capacity-exclusion core assumed

`SBSBridge.lean` proves the amplification chain **conditional** on a per-collision
overlap `γ < 1`:

- `overlap_amplifies` : the joint overlap of a block of `L` *independent* collisions
  is the product `∏ cₐ` of the per-collision overlaps, hence `≤ γ^L`.
- `block_overlap_tendsto_zero` : if `0 ≤ γ < 1` then `γ^L → 0` — weak monitoring is
  amplified into near-perfect (orthogonal) records.

There `γ` was a *hypothesis*. The open question: does an actual measurement-like
**interaction Hamiltonian** produce `γ < 1`? Here we exhibit one and compute `γ`.

## 2. The toy Hamiltonian (Zurek's controlled-monitoring / collisional decoherence model)

A two-outcome pointer `S` (basis `{|0⟩,|1⟩}`, eigenvalues `s = ±1` of `σ_z^S`) is
monitored, one environment qubit at a time, by the **controlled interaction**

> **H_int = g · σ_z^S ⊗ A,**  with `A` a self-adjoint involution on the environment
> qubit (`A* = A`, `A² = 𝟙`) — concretely `A = σ_x^E`.

Two features are *structural*, not assumed by hand:

1. **Einselection / pointer basis is fixed by the Hamiltonian.** `H_int` commutes
   with `σ_z^S` (`[H_int, σ_z^S ⊗ 𝟙] = 0`), so the pointer basis `{|0⟩,|1⟩}` is the
   one the interaction does *not* disturb — exactly the SBS "diagonal in a single
   pointer basis" condition, here derived from `H`.
2. **Branch-dependence.** Conditioned on the pointer eigenvalue `s = ±1`, the
   environment feels the Hamiltonian `H_s = (s g) A`, so the two branches drive the
   environment *differently*.

## 3. The branch-conditioned environment states

Evolving one collision for time `τ` (write `θ := g τ`), the environment qubit (start
state `|ε₀⟩`, a unit vector) is taken by the Schrödinger propagator of `H_s` to

> **U_s(θ) = exp(−i θ H_s) = exp(−i θ s A) = cos θ · 𝟙 − i·s·sin θ · A**   (since `A² = 𝟙`),
>
> **|E_s⟩ = U_s(θ)|ε₀⟩.**

`U_s` is genuinely the Hamiltonian flow: it is a one-parameter group
(`U_s(θ₁)U_s(θ₂) = U_s(θ₁+θ₂)`, `U_s(0)=𝟙`) whose generator is the self-adjoint `H_s`, so
it is unitary by Stone's theorem. *(Machine-checked: `collisionU_group`, `collisionU_zero`,
`hamiltonian_isSymmetric`.)*

For `A = σ_x`, `|ε₀⟩ = |0⟩`: `|E_+⟩ = cos θ|0⟩ − i sin θ|1⟩`,
`|E_−⟩ = cos θ|0⟩ + i sin θ|1⟩`.

## 4. The per-collision overlap — and why it is < 1

The two branch records overlap by

> **⟨E_+ | E_− ⟩ = cos²θ − sin²θ = cos 2θ,**   so   **γ = |cos 2θ|.**

*(Machine-checked: `branch_overlap` gives `⟨E_+,E_−⟩ = cos²θ − sin²θ`.)* The only
input is that `{|ε₀⟩, A|ε₀⟩}` is orthonormal — automatic for `A = σ_x` on `|0⟩`, since
`A` being a self-adjoint involution makes `‖A ε₀‖ = ‖ε₀‖`, and `⟨ε₀, A ε₀⟩ = 0` is the
non-degeneracy of the coupling (the interaction actually moves the environment).

Then **`γ = |cos 2θ| < 1` for every coupling `θ ∉ (π/2)ℤ`** — i.e. for *generic* `gτ`,
the single collision only *partially* distinguishes the outcomes (weak monitoring).
*(Machine-checked: `gamma_lt_one` for `0 < θ < π/4`, with `0 < cos 2θ < 1`; the special
value `θ = π/4` gives `γ = 0`, a one-shot perfect record.)*

This is the whole content of the previously-assumed premise: a concrete, stable
(time-independent `H`), factorized (one qubit per collision), branch-dependent
(`H_s` depends on `s`) interaction yields `γ = |cos 2θ| < 1`.

## 5. Factorization → amplification → records (the existing chain, now unconditional in the model)

Successive collisions act on *fresh, independent* environment qubits, so after `L`
collisions the branch-conditioned environment state is the tensor product
`|E_s⟩^{⊗L}`, and the joint overlap **factorizes**:

> **⟨E_+^{⊗L} | E_−^{⊗L}⟩ = (cos 2θ)^L = γ^L  →  0.**

This is precisely the hypothesis `overlap_amplifies`/`block_overlap_tendsto_zero`
consumed — now *supplied* by the Hamiltonian (`collisional_block_overlap`,
`collisional_overlap_tendsto_zero`). Partitioning `N` collisions into `k = ⌊N/L⌋`
blocks yields `k` near-orthogonal redundant records, i.e. an (approximate)
`ObjectivityWitness` with redundancy `k` — feeding `fragment_finrank_ge_approx` and
the single-record exclusion `sbs_single_outcome`.

## 6. Honest residual (what this does and does not establish)

- **Does:** discharge "γ<1 from a Hamiltonian" for the standard collisional/QND toy
  model — `H_int = g σ_z^S ⊗ σ_x^E` gives `γ = |cos 2θ|`, with einselection of the
  pointer basis and branch-dependence both *consequences* of `H`, not stipulations.
  The propagator is a verified one-parameter unitary group generated by a self-adjoint
  `H_s`.
- **Does not:** establish the premise for a *realistic field-theoretic* scattering
  Hamiltonian, nor that an arbitrary system–environment `H` self-organizes into this
  factorized QND form (the **uniform-γ, no-recoherence, independent-collision**
  structure is the model's input). Promoting this from a toy two-level monitor to a
  field-theoretic derivation — with the uniformity of `γ` across collisions earned
  rather than posited — remains the genuine open physics. What was an opaque "assume
  γ<1" is now "γ = |cos 2θ| for this `H`, and here is exactly which modeling features
  (QND coupling, fresh independent fragments, stable coupling `g`) are still posited."

classical*, RMP 75 (2003); Joos–Zeh collisional decoherence; Korbicz, arXiv:2007.04276
(SBS); the Strasberg–Winter branch-selection framing, arXiv:2601.19703.
