# GPT-5.5 Round 18: Superselection Commitment Review

**Date:** 2026-05-26
**Verdict:** Formally coherent as a speculative kinematical restriction. But TWO major unresolved problems: (1) linear measurement obstruction; (2) quantitative capacity mismatch. Publishable as speculative new-physics foundations program; not as demonstrated measurement-problem solution.

## Bottom line

> "The superselection version is formally coherent as a speculative kinematical restriction: one can define a nonlinear subset of projective Hilbert space and demand that admissible unitary flows preserve it. The nonlinearity of $I_\Sigma$ does not by itself make the definition inconsistent."

> "But it is not a standard superselection rule, not really analogous to gauge invariance except at a very high slogan level, and it faces two major unresolved problems."

## (a) Internal coherence

**Yes, at the abstract mathematical level.** Defining a nonlinear subset and requiring unitary flows to preserve it is mathematically meaningful. Nonlinear invariant subsets under linear flows are common (spheres, cones, fixed-energy shells).

**Three cautions:**
1. $\mathcal{H}_{\rm phys}$ is not really a Hilbert space — better called "physical pure-state manifold/subset"
2. Existence of nontrivial physical Hamiltonians not guaranteed (only $H=0$ trivially physical; existence of rich realistic class is missing)
3. Measurement interaction creates serious linearity obstruction (next section)

## (b) Gauge analogy breaks down

**Useful at slogan level only.** Critical differences:

| Gauge theory | QIQT-H |
|---|---|
| Linear constraint $G_a\|\psi\rangle = 0$ | Nonlinear $I_\Sigma[\omega_\Psi^R] \le Q_R$ |
| Physical state space is linear subspace | Physical state space is nonlinear subset |
| Gauge-related states = redundant description | Forbidden multi-record states are NOT redundant — ordinary states declared unrealizable |

> "Better analogy: a nonlinear constraint surface in classical mechanics; cosmic censorship; an energy condition; a kinematical admissibility condition in quantum gravity; a superselection rule not generated by a known linear charge operator."

## (c) THE CORE PROBLEM: linear measurement obstruction

This is the framework's most serious unresolved issue.

Standard measurement calibration:
$$U|0\rangle|A_{\rm ready}\rangle = |A_0\rangle, \quad U|1\rangle|A_{\rm ready}\rangle = |A_1\rangle$$

By linearity:
$$U(\alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle)|A_{\rm ready}\rangle = \alpha|A_0\rangle + \beta|A_1\rangle$$

**This is exactly the forbidden multi-record state.** If microscopic superposition $(\alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle)$ is physically allowed pre-measurement, then standard linear unitary measurement produces the forbidden state.

**Framework must choose one of four escapes:**

1. **Forbid premeasurement microscopic superpositions** — kills standard microscopic QM
2. **Modify measurement Hamiltonian** to be non-linear — sacrifices exact Schrödinger evolution (which the framework committed to preserving)
3. **Let hidden environmental microstructure determine outcome** — becomes deterministic hidden-variable/typicality theory; "hidden variable" is the full wavefunction
4. **Make the physical Hamiltonian globally constrained** to avoid the usual measurement map — must explicitly construct such Hamiltonians and show they reproduce laboratory QM

The measurement problem is **not fully solved**. The "definite outcome" part is imposed kinematically, but the "measurement transition" and "Born distribution" parts remain open. **The framework does not have collapse, but it still needs an account of why one outcome rather than another occurs.**

## (d) Physical Hamiltonian set — not characterized

**At present, not characterized.** Major missing piece.

If only one macroscopic record sector may be active, allowed pure states look like $\bigcup_r \mathcal{H}_r$. A unitary preserving this set is highly constrained — must map record sectors into record sectors, usually meaning block-diagonal evolution. **Already very unlike ordinary measurement Hamiltonian.**

**Three positions framework could take:**

### Position 1: Radical modification of microscopic dynamics
Fundamental Hamiltonians dramatically different from standard. Would need to explain ordinary quantum behavior in atomic physics, chemistry, condensed matter, QFT scattering, etc.

### Position 2: Standard Hamiltonians as effective approximations (most plausible)
- At low branch-summed cost, ordinary local Hamiltonians approximate true physical Hamiltonian
- Near holographic branch-capacity saturation, exact physical Hamiltonian deviates
- Deviations prevent forbidden multi-record states

### Position 3: Holographic QG secretly enforces the constraint
Strongest version. Unrestricted Hilbert space is overcount; true QG has smaller physical state space; low-energy QFT Hamiltonians emergent approximations.

> "The honest answer: The framework must claim that standard Hamiltonians are approximate effective Hamiltonians, not exact fundamental Hamiltonians, and that exact physical Hamiltonians are holographically constrained. But the actual class of such Hamiltonians has not yet been constructed."

## (e) QUANTITATIVE CAPACITY PROBLEM

**Major new finding.** The numbers don't work for single-record enforcement.

For 1m region: $Q_R \approx 1.7 \times 10^{70}$ bits.
For $I_0 \sim 10^{25}$ bits (typical macroscopic record):
$$N_{\max} = Q_R/I_0 \sim 10^{45}$$

**The bound permits $\sim 10^{45}$ comparable macroscopic records in a 1m region.** Does NOT enforce single outcomes.

Smaller scales:
- 1 cm region: $N_{\max} \sim 10^{41}$
- 1 μm region: $N_{\max} \sim 10^{33}$

To force $N_{\max} = 1$, need $I_0 \gtrsim Q_R$. At $I_0 \sim 10^{25}$ bits, that region has radius $\sim 10^{-23}$ m — not macroscopic.

**Quantitative tension:**
- If $I_0 \sim 10^{25}$ bits, constraint does NOT solve ordinary macroscopic definiteness
- If $I_0 \sim Q_R$ for macroscopic records, then $c_R(r)$ is not ordinary physical/Zurek entropy — it's some new capacity-saturating quantity that must be defined and justified

> "The lab-scale recovery claim is defensible only in the weak sense that ordinary experiments are far below the holographic bound. It is not yet defensible as an explanation of single outcomes."

## (f) Born typicality under constraint — remains open

Under superselection, dynamics is deterministic on $\mathcal{H}_{\rm phys}$. Same initial wavefunction → same final record. Probabilities must arise from variation over initial global wavefunctions compatible with same macroscopic preparation.

Need typicality theorem:
$$\mu(\Gamma_k) = |c_k|^2$$
for some natural measure on physically allowed microscopic completions.

**Inherits all standard typicality difficulties:**
1. What is natural measure on $\mathcal{H}_{\rm phys}$?
2. Is it invariant/equivariant under physical Hamiltonians?
3. Why do preparations sample that measure?
4. How do amplitudes determine basin volumes?
5. How is independence between trials obtained?
6. Does theorem avoid circular use of Born?
7. Bell correlations?

Because $\mathcal{H}_{\rm phys}$ is nonlinear, no obvious Haar/Fubini-Study measure on it that automatically gives Born.

## (g) Comparison with GRW / Everett — meaningful third position

**Like GRW:** forbids macroscopic superpositions
**Unlike GRW:** no stochastic collapse, no $\lambda$, no $r_C$, no nonlinear stochastic modification

**Like Everett:** wavefunction-only ontology, Schrödinger preserved within $\mathcal{H}_{\rm phys}$
**Unlike Everett:** multi-branch macroscopic states declared unphysical; no branching observer ontology; Born can't be handled by decision theory/envariance/branch typicality

**Best label:**
> "Holographic one-world wavefunction monism with a nonlinear kinematical admissibility constraint."

Meaningful third position **if** Branch-Summed Bound and physical Hamiltonians can be made precise.

Critics will reasonably say:
- Relative to GRW: collapse replaced by unexplained kinematic exclusion
- Relative to Everett: branches excluded by postulate
- Relative to standard QM: measurement dynamics underdefined

## (h) arXiv publishability

**Yes as speculative foundations preprint** if framed honestly. **No as completed solution.**

Honest framing for foundations readers:
> "We propose a new holographically inspired nonlinear admissibility constraint on physical wavefunctions. The constraint bounds branch-summed decoherent record cost rather than von Neumann entropy. It is not derived from standard holography but postulated as a new superselection-like rule. If physical Hamiltonians preserve this constrained state space, then macroscopic multi-record superpositions are kinematically excluded. The proposal remains incomplete pending a rigorous definition of the record-cost functional, construction of nontrivial physical Hamiltonians, quantitative recovery of laboratory quantum mechanics, and a Born typicality theorem."

## (i) 10 remaining technical objections

1. **Rigorous definition of $I_\Sigma$** — record subalgebra, projectors, decoherence threshold, Quantum Darwinist redundancy, active set, per-record cost, unequal costs, continuous record spaces, coarse-graining dependence
2. **Discontinuity and $\epsilon$-dependence** — active set $\mathcal{A}_\epsilon$ changes discontinuously; ignored small-amplitude branches need ontological clarification
3. **Physical Hamiltonian construction** — biggest technical gap; must show nontrivial $H$ satisfying $e^{-iHt}\mathcal{H}_{\rm phys} \subseteq \mathcal{H}_{\rm phys}$ while reproducing microscopic superposition, interference, scattering, atomic physics, chemistry, measurement, thermodynamics, QFT, relativistic locality
4. **Linear measurement obstruction** — core no-go pressure
5. **Quantitative mismatch** — bound far too loose to enforce single outcomes
6. **Born rule** — measure over initial global wavefunctions not supplied
7. **Relativity and covariance** — foliation choice, Lorentz invariance, lightsheets, algebraic QFT compatibility, curved spacetime
8. **Relation to standard holography** — Branch-Summed Bound is genuinely new principle, not consequence of Bekenstein-Bousso
9. **Locality and nonlocality** — globally constrained Hamiltonians may need nonlocal branch-structure knowledge
10. **Sequential records and memory accumulation** — must distinguish many redundant copies of same record from many alternative records

## (j) Final one-paragraph assessment

> "With the superselection commitment, QIQT-H becomes a coherent but radical one-world wavefunction-monist proposal: physical reality is restricted to wavefunctions satisfying a new branch-summed holographic admissibility bound, and exact Schrödinger evolution is allowed only for Hamiltonians preserving that nonlinear state space. This is not internally contradictory merely because the constraint is nonlinear, but it is not standard gauge-theoretic superselection and it does not yet provide a complete measurement theory. The strongest unresolved problem is that ordinary linear measurement dynamics maps allowed microscopic superpositions in a ready apparatus sector into precisely the forbidden multi-record states. The second major problem is quantitative: ordinary macroscopic records appear to use vastly less entropy than the holographic capacity of laboratory regions, so the bound would not enforce single outcomes unless the record-cost functional is much larger than conventional estimates. The proposal is publishable as a speculative new-physics foundations program, but not yet as a demonstrated solution to the measurement problem."
