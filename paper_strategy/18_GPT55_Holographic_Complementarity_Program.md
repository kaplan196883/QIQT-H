# GPT-5.5 Evaluation of Holographic Complementarity Research Program

**Date:** 2026-05-24
**Verdict:** Promising research direction. Not a finished theory. Requires honest reframing.

## The honest reframe

> "The framework does not keep global single-world realism. It replaces it with causal-diamond-relative single-world realism."

This is the critical move. The trilemma is not strictly escaped — it's resolved by relaxing "global single-world realism" to "perspectival single-world realism per causal diamond." The framework is honestly closer to RQM, Everett relative states, and decoherent histories than to a position that keeps all three original commitments simultaneously.

## The genuine novelty

> "The sharpest distinction from RQM would be: (1) facts are not merely 'relative to systems,' but to causal-diamond holographic screens; (2) the dimension/information capacity of each observer's fact-space is bounded by area; (3) overlaps obey explicit algebraic or Hilbert-space consistency maps; (4) the framework is intended as an ontology of quantum gravity, not merely an interpretation of ordinary QM."

That's where the novelty sits. Not in observer-relative facts (Rovelli, Everett, modal interpretations all have this) but in the **specific causal-diamond / holographic structure** and the **explicit overlap consistency**.

## Literature connections identified (substantial)

**Closest technical relatives:**
- **Banks-Fischler holographic space-time (HST)** — finite-dim Hilbert spaces assigned to causal diamonds with overlap consistency
- **Algebraic QFT (Haag-Kastler nets)** — natural mathematical language for causal-diamond-local ontology
- **Decoherent histories** (Gell-Mann-Hartle, Griffiths, Omnès, Halliwell) — single-history readings are very close in spirit
- **Adrian Kent's Lorentzian quantum reality program** — most serious existing attempt to build Lorentz-compatible realist quantum ontology
- **Rovelli's RQM and Bene-Dieks perspectival modal interpretations** — the philosophical kin
- **Holographic quantum error correction** (HaPPY code, JLMS, Almheiri-Dong-Harlow) — the technical tools for complementarity

**Wigner-friend no-go literature to engage:**
- Frauchiger-Renner
- Brukner's no-go theorem for observer-independent facts
- Bong et al. local friendliness inequalities
- Cavalcanti-Wiseman

## The four options for what λ_O actually is

GPT-5.5 identifies four candidates:

1. **λ_O as state on causal-diamond algebra** — doesn't produce definite outcomes (still quantum state)
2. **λ_O as valuation on decohered record subalgebra** — *probably the best route*. Each diamond contains a decoherence-selected approximately commutative record subalgebra $\mathcal{C}(D) \subset \mathcal{A}(D)$, and λ_O is a valuation assigning definite values only to stable macroscopic records. Avoids Kochen-Specker.
3. **λ_O as selected decoherent history** — connects naturally to decoherent histories machinery
4. **λ_O as holographic boundary data** — most quantum-gravitational; requires causal-diamond holography we don't fully have

The valuation-on-record-subalgebra option (Option 2) is most tractable for a first paper.

## The required honesty about Bell

> "Bell's theorem implies that your theory cannot satisfy all of: outcome definiteness, measurement independence, locality/factorization, single joint hidden variable in the common past. ... The framework is Lorentz-covariant but not Bell-local. It preserves no-signalling, not local causality."

This must be stated explicitly. The framework is Lorentz-covariant in its formulation but inherits the standard Bell-nonlocality of any single-world hidden-variable theory.

## The minimum viable first paper

GPT-5.5 proposes a five-section structure:

**Title candidate:** *"Causal-Diamond Perspectival Realism: Observer-Relative Actuality, Holographic Compatibility, and the Measurement Problem."*

**Five things the paper should do:**

1. **State the no-go motivation honestly.** Global single-world realism + strict unitarity + Lorentz covariance is obstructed by Bell, Wigner-friend, and relativistic causal structure.

2. **Define the formal ontology.** For each causal diamond $D$:
   - $\mathcal{A}(D)$ — quantum algebra of observables on $D$
   - $\mathcal{C}(D) \subset \mathcal{A}(D)$ — decohered record subalgebra
   - $\lambda_D \in \mathrm{Spec}(\mathcal{C}(D))$ — actual record state
   - Overlap consistency: $\lambda_D|_{D \cap D'} = \lambda_{D'}|_{D \cap D'}$ when a common record algebra is defined

3. **Define Born consistency.** A measure $\mu_D^\Psi(\lambda_D)$ reproducing Born statistics for all record projectors in $\mathcal{C}(D)$, with marginal consistency under coarse-graining or inclusion.

4. **Work through toy examples.** Wigner's friend, Bell, Frauchiger-Renner. Show where contradictions are blocked. Show that Bell nonlocality appears as nonfactorizable consistency between future joint records, not as superluminal signalling.

5. **Clarify relation to RQM and Everett.** Acknowledge that the framework is perspectival. The claimed novelty is the causal-diamond/holographic structure and explicit overlap algebra, not the mere idea of observer-relative facts.

## Feasibility assessment

- **As full quantum-gravity theory:** not feasible in a few years
- **As foundations/QG-interface program:** feasible if scoped carefully
- **Near-term path:** axiomatic or toy-model formulation, NOT derivation from quantum gravity
- **Test cases:** Wigner's friend, Bell/EPR, delayed-choice, observers separated by horizons, simple holographic code toy models

## Bottom-line recommendation

> "I would pursue it, but not as 'we solve the measurement problem using holography.' That will invite justified skepticism. Pursue it as: 'A causal-diamond perspectival ontology for unitary quantum theory, motivated by holographic complementarity.'"

The framework is publishable as a research-program paper that formalizes causal-diamond record algebras, demonstrates consistency in standard paradoxes, and engages the relevant existing literatures honestly. The main danger is that it collapses into RQM/Everett/decoherent-histories unless the causal-diamond structure does real technical work.
