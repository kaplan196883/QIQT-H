# GPT-5.5 Steelman Defense of Info-Cost Framework

**Date:** 2026-05-24

## The opening concession

> "The original paper cannot survive if its 'information-cost theorem' is read as a theorem of ordinary Hilbert-space linear algebra. In a fixed Hilbert space, a superposition of two vectors is just another vector. So the defense must shift the claim from **abstract Hilbert-space representability** to **physical representability as a semiclassical, holographically admissible record-state inside one causal domain**."

This is the critical move. The defense gives up the strict "2Q_R" arithmetic and reframes "information cost" as **record-complexity / coherence-maintenance cost / geometric code-subspace cost**.

## The reformulated central thesis (defensible)

> "Macroscopic superpositions are cheap as formal vectors but extraordinarily expensive, perhaps impossible, as stable semiclassical record-states under holographic constraints."

This is a weaker but defensible claim. It says: the formal Hilbert-space vector exists; the *physical instantiation* of that vector as a stable, redundantly-encoded, environmentally-imprinted, semiclassical record structure is what's bounded.

## The framework rescued — six key revisions

### 1. Replace "Hilbert dimension doubling" with "record-complexity bound"

The claim is not that superposition doubles dim. It is that a *single semiclassical world* containing two macroscopically distinct record-webs requires maintaining global phase coherence across astronomical degrees of freedom, plus encoding two mutually incompatible classical correlation structures. This is the cost that exceeds holographic capacity, not the abstract Hilbert dimension.

### 2. FQ + 3 additional ingredients for basis selection

FQ alone doesn't select the pointer basis. The defense needs:

- **(FQ)** Holographic entropy bound
- **Locality / semi-classicality** — physical worlds support stable spacetime records
- **Decoherence / quantum Darwinism** (Zurek) — pointer bases are those with environmental redundancy
- **Holographic record admissibility** — record structures must fit within finite boundary information budget

The cat-basis states $(|alive\rangle \pm |dead\rangle)/\sqrt{2}$ are valid formal vectors but are not "robust macroscopic record states" — they fail the locality + redundancy + admissibility test, not the entropy bound alone.

### 3. Dynamics MUST be specified — two routes

The framework cannot simultaneously have (unitary per-trial dynamics + no macroscopic superpositions per-trial + single outcomes + complete wave function). One must give. Two coherent options:

**Route A: Objective collapse.** Stochastic non-unitary transition when superposition would form a macroscopic record-superposition. Collapse threshold = holographic record complexity. Sacrifices exact per-trial unitarity.

**Route B: Bohmian-style hidden-variable actualism.** Keep unitary wave-function evolution. Add a primitive ontology: actual record structure. The wave function may contain formal branches, but only one is *ontically occupied* as the actual world. The hidden variable is a microscopic holographic state $\lambda$, not particle positions.

The defense: pick one. Be explicit about it.

### 4. Typicality needs an equivariance theorem (Bohmian model)

The Born-from-typicality argument is currently a promissory note. To make it real:

- Specify a finite/effectively-finite holographic phase space $\Lambda$ of microscopic per-trial realizations
- Specify a measure $\mu_\Psi(\lambda)$
- Specify a dynamical flow $T_t$ on $\Lambda$
- **Prove equivariance:** $\mu_{\Psi_t}(T_t^{-1} B) = \mu_{\Psi_0}(B)$
- Show Born weights $|c_i|^2$ emerge as record-frequencies

Bohmian mechanics has genuine equivariance for $|\psi|^2$. The holographic framework needs the analogous structure for record outcomes.

### 5. Embrace the Bohmian comparison

> "The hidden-variable layer is not an embarrassment. It is the price of realism plus single outcomes. If one rejects many-worlds and rejects fundamental collapse, then some additional variable or actualization rule is needed."

Reframe as **Bohmian-style holographic theory**:
- **Wave function:** ensemble-level / nomological, evolves unitarily
- **Hidden actual state $\lambda$:** microscopic holographic state on boundary Hilbert space or finite-dim causal-diamond state space (NOT particle positions)
- **Macroscopic records:** emergent quasiclassical structures determined by $\lambda$ and guided by $\Psi$

This is more than Ballentine — it connects ensemble interpretation to quantum-gravity constraints. The distinctive claim: the hidden variables are holographically constrained microscopic structures, not particle positions in $3N$-dim configuration space.

### 6. Mandelbrot analogy — reformulate as physical instantiation

> "The mathematical expression of a state and the physical instantiation of a record-state are different things. Holographic limits constrain the latter, not the former."

The analogy is defensible if it's about **finite physical instantiation in a bounded region of spacetime**, not about **representation by finite computation**.

## The structural defense (D)

The irreducible core that survives:

> "The universal wave function, considered as an abstract Hilbert-space object, should not automatically be identified with a physically instantiated multiplicity of macroscopic worlds. Physical worlds are definite, quasiclassical record structures, and the space of such structures is constrained by holographic information bounds."

The defensible thesis: *macroscopic superpositions may be formal vectors in Hilbert space, but they are not automatically admissible as single semiclassical record-realities.*

## The Bohmian-comparison defense (E)

A "Bohmian-style holographic theory" has three layers:
1. Wave function (nomological)
2. Hidden actual state $\lambda$ (microscopic holographic, not particles)
3. Macroscopic records (emergent from $\lambda$ guided by $\Psi$)

Measurement: $\Psi \to \sum_i c_i \Psi_i$. The actual $\lambda$ enters one record sector $R_i$. Born probabilities arise if the equilibrium distribution satisfies $\mu(R_i) = |c_i|^2$.

This is structurally analogous to Bohmian mechanics but with:
- "Actual record structure" replacing "actual particle configuration"
- Boundary-encoded microstructure replacing bulk particle positions in 3N space
- Holographic motivation: if finite boundary data are fundamental, the hidden variable is boundary-encoded

A plausible quantum-gravity-inspired hidden-variable program.

## The hill worth dying on (F)

The minimal sustainable claims:

1. Do not deny Hilbert-space superposition (macroscopic superpositions are valid formal vectors)
2. Deny that every formal vector corresponds to a single semiclassical world
3. Define macroscopic reality in terms of stable records
4. Use holography as a constraint on record-instantiation, not as a basis selector by itself
5. Add an explicit one-world ontology (either collapse or hidden variables)
6. Treat the Born rule as a needed theorem or postulate — don't pretend typicality is derived

**The hill worth dying on (one sentence):**

> *Quantum mechanics supplies an ensemble-level Hilbert-space formalism. Individual physical reality consists of one definite quasiclassical record-world. Holographic entropy bounds constrain which record-worlds can be physically instantiated in a finite causal domain. Macroscopic superpositions exist as formal wave-function structures, but not as simultaneously realized worlds. To complete the theory, one must add either a collapse dynamics or a Bohmian-style hidden-variable/actual-record dynamics with a Born-compatible typicality measure.*

This is a coherent, defensible research program — not a finished theorem.
