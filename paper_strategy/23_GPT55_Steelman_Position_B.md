## The core steelman

> "Kapłański's framework should be understood as a **holographic finite-precision completion of unitary quantum mechanics**."

It does not deny Hilbert-space formalism. It does not modify Schrödinger. No GRW jumps, Bohmian particles, or many real branches. Central move:

> "The Hilbert-space continuum is a mathematical idealization. A bounded region of spacetime cannot physically instantiate an infinite amount of information. Therefore the wave function, if treated as a physical state of spacetime, cannot possess infinite physical specification precision."

Result: exact unitary field-algebra evolution + single outcomes + no collapse + no MWI ontology + no Bohmian particles + no modal value rule + no QBist subjectivity + direct conceptual link to holography, black-hole thermodynamics, and finite information capacity of spacetime.

## (a) The literal reading IS the natural reading

The key argument: the narrow entanglement-entropy reading leaves an absurdity:

> "1. A bounded region is said to contain at most finite physical entropy/information.
> 2. But the exact amplitudes of its wave function are still treated as physically real real numbers containing infinitely many digits.
> 3. Therefore the region secretly contains infinite physical information in the real-number specification of its state.
> That cannot be the intended lesson of holography."

Bekenstein, 't Hooft, Susskind, Bousso, Banks — they're all making physical claims about state capacity, not just technical entropy inequalities. The narrow reading is "too weak."

> "If the wave function is ontic — if it is a real physical state of spacetime — then its amplitudes are not exempt from physical information bounds. They are not magical Platonic quantities floating outside the region whose state they describe. They are part of the physical specification of the region."

This converts holography from a black-hole entropy slogan into a foundational principle for QM.

## (b) The per-run concentration claim is coherent

The decoherence objection assumes "the formal ensemble wave function is literally the exact per-run physical wave function. But that assumption is precisely what the finite-information postulate denies."

**Classical statistical mechanics analogy:**
> "When one says a gas has temperature $T$, one is not saying every individual molecule has the same energy. The macrostate is an equivalence class over many microstates. Likewise, when one says a qubit has state $\alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle$, one may be describing an equivalence class of microscopic physical preparations, not an infinitely precise ontic vector shared identically by every run."

In a specific run with definite microscopic IC, the measurement amplification cascade gives definite outcomes. The ensemble state contains both possibilities because it averages over the microscopic IC ensemble. **The individual run need not contain two physically real macroscopic records.**

> "Kapłański is not saying: Add mysterious variables to quantum mechanics.
> He is saying: Stop pretending the formal Hilbert vector used in the ensemble description is the infinitely precise physical state of every individual run."

The framework's claim is:
> "Decoherence plus microscopic initial-condition specificity plus finite physical amplitude precision solves the single-record problem without modifying unitary dynamics."

## (c) The qubit defense made strongest

> "The abstract Bloch sphere is to the physical qubit what Euclidean geometry is to a real measuring rod: an idealization. Useful, elegant, and extraordinarily accurate — but not literally instantiated with infinite precision."

A physical qubit is always implemented in a substrate (ion, atom, photon, superconducting circuit, etc.) with finite size, energy, control precision, and spacetime information capacity. **Cannot physically encode infinitely precise real number in amplitude ratio.**

The holographic bound makes this *principled, not merely practical*. Even in principle, bounded systems cannot contain unlimited distinguishable information. **Two Bloch vectors separated by less than physical info resolution are the same physical state.**

Error correction doesn't evade this — larger substrate = larger capacity but still finite.

## (d) Bell: reject measurement independence via holographic nonseparability

The cleanest Bell escape route: reject $\rho(\lambda | a,b) = \rho(\lambda)$.

**Not classical conspiratorial superdeterminism.** Rather:
> "A Bell experiment is not composed of three independently specifiable pieces: source hidden variables, Alice's freely chosen setting, Bob's freely chosen setting. Rather, the entire experiment is one finite-information physical process embedded in a common spacetime history."

Holography provides principled grounds to reject classical separability:
> "In quantum gravity, bounded regions do not factor into independent tensor products in the naive way. The information content of a region is constrained globally by boundary area. The algebraic structure is not that of independently specifiable local classical variables sitting in spacetime."

> "Bell's measurement-independence assumption is not a theorem of physics. It is an additional classical independence postulate. Holographic quantum mechanics has no obligation to satisfy it."

The framework "denies that this counterfactual is physically legitimate":
> "The exact same hidden-variable distribution at the source could have occurred with arbitrarily different physically instantiated later settings."

Operational freedom intact. No signaling. No empirical freedom lost. Only the metaphysical counterfactual fails.

**This preserves the framework's major virtues:** no preferred foliation, no explicit nonlocal collapse, no Bohmian action-at-a-distance, exact unitary evolution, Lorentz-covariant info bounds, single outcomes.

Plus: microscopic IC structure + rejection of Bell measurement independence (in strong classical sense).

But these prices are **independently motivated by holography, black-hole entropy, and quantum gravity** — not invented solely to solve the measurement problem.

From which measurement-theoretic consequences follow.

**The hidden inconsistency this resolves:** The traditional measurement problem arises because we illegitimately combine (1) exact continuum Hilbert-space formalism, (2) ontic reading of WF, (3) finite physical universe.
