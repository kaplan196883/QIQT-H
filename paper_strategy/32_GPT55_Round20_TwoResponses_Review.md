## (a) Operational answer to linearity — defensible, relocates question

> "The response is legitimate in one important sense: if QIQT-H denies that the unrestricted Hilbert-space vector is physically primary, then the standard objection cannot simply be imposed from outside. A theory is allowed to say: 'That linear extension exists in the formal completion of Hilbert space, but not every formal vector is a physically admissible state.' In that sense, the author has a real reply to the naive linearity objection."

**Three remaining technical issues:**

1. **Standard superselection doesn't select one sector** — only removes coherences. Mixtures over sectors are allowed in ordinary superselection. QIQT-H wants something stronger: even the diagonal mixture is forbidden as an ontic state. Must distinguish carefully between ontic state, epistemic density matrix, and ensemble distribution.

2. **Single-record states don't form a linear subspace.** If $|S_0\rangle, |S_1\rangle$ are allowed but $\alpha|S_0\rangle + \beta|S_1\rangle$ is not, then $\mathcal{H}_{\rm phys}$ is a nonlinear submanifold. "Hamiltonian" cannot be ordinary linear self-adjoint operator on that space. Needs nonlinear deterministic dynamics, stochastic dynamics, hidden variables, modal-style selection rule, or new finite-precision algebraic dynamics.

3. **Decoherence + FQ gives mixtures, not outcomes.** Solves coherence problem, not actuality problem. Still need a rule saying which diagonal component is realized per run.

> "The response works as a rebuttal to the simplest Hilbert-space linearity objection. It does not yet work as a complete account of single outcomes. It relocates the question from 'why not linear superposition?' to 'what exactly is the finite-precision constrained dynamics that selects one record?'"

## (b) $I_0$-as-experimental-parameter — solves crude mismatch but needs sharpening

**Solves the numerical mismatch** by removing $I_0$ from naive Shannon/thermodynamic counting. Becomes phenomenological cost calibrated empirically. **Legitimate maneuver in principle** — analogous to GRW's $\lambda, r_C$.

**Major caveat:** GRW gives an *explicit dynamical law* once $\lambda, r_C$ are specified. QIQT-H must give comparable specificity. **Six things needed for $I_0$ to be sharp:**

1. Precise definition of record type $r$
2. Rule for computing $c_R(r)$
3. Rule for choosing region $R$
4. Rule for counting branches $N$
5. Rule for treating small-amplitude branches
6. Rule for transferring calibrated $I_0$ between experimental contexts

> "If $I_0$ is one universal constant, or a small family of constants with a principled dependence on physical features of the record, then QIQT-H has empirical content. If instead each experimental class gets its own adjustable $I_0$, the theory becomes too plastic."

## (d) Lab-scale recovery — defensible for microscopic, conditional for measurement

**Microscopic systems** (atoms, photons, ions, qubits, small molecules): safe — should behave according to ordinary QM as long as they don't generate large macroscopic record structures.

**Measurement detectors:** depends on what "record cost" means. If detector screen has only $10^{20}$–$10^{25}$ DOF and regional bound is $10^{70}$ bits, then $I_0$ cannot simply be ordinary info content — must be new kind of branch/record cost.

**Region-dependence issue:** $Q_R$ depends on $A(\partial R)$, which is chosen by the theorist. Need principled prescription: causal diamond, apparatus region, minimal enclosing screen, environmental redundancy domain, etc. Predictions can't depend arbitrarily on chosen boundary.

## (e) Empirical calibration program — real but not yet operationally well-defined

**Sensible at high level.** But needs:

1. **What counts as a record?** (Pointer state? Redundantly encoded environment? Thermodynamic imprint? Coarse-grained algebra eigenstate?)
2. **How is $N$ counted?** (Two-outcome obvious; continuous variables? Chaotic environments? Tiny branches?)
3. **Do branch weights matter?** ($10^{-1000}$ amplitude counts equally?)
4. **How is $R$ chosen?** Predictions invariant under enlargement.
5. **Is $I_0$ universal or system-dependent?** If latter, what determines it?
6. **How does $I_0$ compose?** Additivity? Subadditivity?
7. **What is the boundary law?** Sharp at $N \cdot I_0 = Q_R$? Crossover? Visibility vanishes discontinuously or continuously?

> "The program is concrete enough to motivate work, but not yet operationally well-defined."

## (f) Born typicality — still a promissory note (major unresolved)

> "This remains one of the largest unresolved problems."

Same structure as Bohmian typicality, but QIQT-H lacks the analogue of Bohm's $|\psi|^2$ measure on configuration space. Central questions:

1. What are microscopic initial conditions? Quantum states of environment? What measure on that space?
2. Why should that measure produce $|c_k|^2$? (If postulated, circular)
3. Is the measure equivariant under constrained dynamics?
4. Sequential probabilities for repeated/incompatible measurements?
5. Bell experiments — how does deterministic single-outcome theory reproduce quantum correlations?
6. Still ψ-monism? If "different microscopic IC" are additional facts not in WF, the theory has hidden variables.

> "The typicality program is not yet clean. It is a plausible place to try to recover Born statistics, but at present it is a promissory note."

Combination of commitments is unusual:
