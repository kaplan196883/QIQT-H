REALITY CHECK — where λ's holographic bit limit actually sits.

  1 cm² boundary         Q_R ≈ 1.4e+65 bits   →  up to 2^Q_R distinguishable records
  1 m² boundary          Q_R ≈ 1.4e+69 bits   →  up to 2^Q_R distinguishable records
  Earth (4πR²)           Q_R ≈ 7.2e+83 bits   →  up to 2^Q_R distinguishable records
  cosmological horizon   Q_R ≈ 8.5e+121 bits   →  up to 2^Q_R distinguishable records
  --- vs physical resolution ---
  A decohered pointer cell (thermal de Broglie / scattering) is ~10s of orders
  of magnitude COARSER than the holographic ℏ-cap, yet finite. So in practice
  λ indexes DECOHERENCE-defined cells (far fewer than 2^Q_R); the holographic
  bit-limit is a slack in-principle ceiling — it bites only near horizons /
  the Planck scale. The universe runs at ~10^-18 of its holographic capacity.
