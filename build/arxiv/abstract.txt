We propose a foundational framework for quantum mechanics resting on the hypothesis that bounded spacetime regions carry only finite physical information. Building on algebraic quantum field theory and the Chandrasekaran-Penington-Witten / Witten crossed-product construction (which dresses the Type III_1 local algebra of a region into a Type II algebra with a semifinite trace and a finite renormalized entropy), we introduce a finite-information axiom (FQ): the state induced by a per-run wave function on the gravitationally dressed regional algebra has renormalized entropy bounded by the holographic capacity Q_R = A/(4 l_P^2). We distinguish the formal wave function of textbook quantum mechanics from the per-run wave function, whose regional physical content is its state on the regional Type II algebra. Conjecturally (the Macroscopic Definiteness Conjecture), a regional content carrying two or more macroscopically distinct records exceeds Q_R and is not an instantiable physical state; if this holds, then after decoherence the per-run regional content is single-record, the global evolution remains exactly unitary, and no collapse, branches, particles, or value rules are added. Born statistics from typicality of microscopic initial conditions is identified as a key open problem. The deductive core is machine-verified in Lean 4 / Mathlib, and a companion formalization independently checks the bounded modular and coherent-state relative-entropy calculus underlying the regional cost functional. We frame the construction as a research program with explicit conjectures and open problems rather than a completed interpretation.
