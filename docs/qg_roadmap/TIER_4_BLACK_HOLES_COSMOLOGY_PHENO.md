# Tier 4 — Black holes, cosmology, and empirical signatures

> **Goal.** Subject the (hypothetical) completed Tier-2 substrate + Tier-3 emergence to the **hard
> tests** every quantum-gravity theory must pass, and extract whatever **empirical** consequences the
> finite-capacity structure allows. These are the criteria by which the program would actually be
> judged a theory of quantum gravity rather than a formal exercise. The deep tests are category **(c)**
> (they presuppose Tier 2+3); the phenomenology deliverable (§4.5) is genuinely **(a)** and can — and
> should — be done *now*, independent of the rest.

**Ordering note.** §4.1–4.4 are *consequences* of a finished Tier 2+3 and cannot be honestly attempted
before it; listing them is about defining the acceptance tests, not scheduling work. §4.5 is the
exception: it is a near-term, standalone honesty deliverable that prevents vague predictive claims.

---

## 4.1 Black-hole microstate counting  *(category (c))*

**The bar.** Susskind–Uglum / Sakharov coefficient matching (which gives QIQT-H's `1/4` *ratio*) is
**not** microstate counting — it is a renormalization / induced-gravity *consistency* result. A
Tier-4-grade theory must **identify or count the actual finite state space** responsible for
black-hole entropy `S = A/4G`, and show its `log(count)` equals the area/4G generalized entropy. The
finite-capacity postulate is *suggestive* here (a horizon region has finitely many distinguishable
records), but turning suggestion into a counting theorem requires the Tier-2 substrate's explicit
spectrum. **Never present the `1/4` ratio as if it were microstate counting** (ledger: ratio =
`DERIVED-RATIO`; microstate count = `OPEN`).

## 4.2 The information paradox / Page curve  *(category (c))*

Reproduce a **unitary Page curve** for an evaporating black hole — the island/QES mechanism in
holographic terms, or its finite-substrate replacement. QIQT-H's single-world (Φ, λ) ontology + finite
capacity must be shown *consistent* with unitary evaporation and with the entropy bound that forces the
turnover. This is a sharp internal-consistency test: a finite-capacity horizon *should* naturally cap
entropy, but demonstrating the full curve requires emergent dynamical geometry (Tier 3).

## 4.3 Singularity resolution  *(category (c))*

Show what replaces the classical singularity when geometry is a finite-capacity quantum object —
curvature bounded by the capacity cutoff, a maximal-density "Planck core," or a substrate phase
transition. Finite capacity is a natural regulator candidate; making it a theorem is Tier-2/3-deep.

## 4.4 Cosmology  *(category (c))*

- The **cosmological constant**: the current roadmap *carries* `Λ` and higher-curvature terms; a real
  UV theory must explain why the observed `Λ` is small and why higher-curvature terms are suppressed.
  This is the cosmological-constant problem — `(c)`, and notoriously unsolved.
- Early-universe / horizon-scale finite-capacity effects, if any, that could leave imprints.

## 4.5 Parameterized phenomenology & bounds  *(category (a); DO THIS NOW — standalone)*

**The honesty deliverable.** *Before* promising a "neutrino-decoherence hook" or "Planck-scale
finite-capacity signature," write the most general **low-energy effective deformation** of standard
dynamics compatible with:
- complete positivity,
- no-signalling,
- approximate Lorentz covariance,
- energy-momentum constraints,
- existing oscillation / interferometry / clock data.

Then **derive the bounds.** The likely (and valuable) outcome: most candidate effects are either
**already tightly constrained** or **Planck-suppressed**. That is a *useful, publishable, honest*
result — and it forecloses vague "finite capacity predicts decoherence" claims that would damage
credibility. Builds directly on `paper_strategy/08_Neutrino_Decoherence_Prediction.md`.

**Deliverable.** A short paper `docs/qg_roadmap/tier4_pheno/` (+ a parameter-bound table) giving the
deformation Lagrangian/master-equation, the symmetry/positivity constraints, and the current
experimental bounds on each coefficient. No claim of a *confirmed* prediction — only a constrained
parameter space and where (if anywhere) finite capacity could in principle show up.

## Tier 4 honest scale

§4.1–4.4 are the **acceptance tests of a finished theory** — all `(c)`, all gated on Tier 2+3, none
attemptable in isolation. They belong here so that "is QIQT-H quantum gravity?" has a concrete,
pre-registered answer key. §4.5 is the **one near-term item**: a parameterized-bounds paper is
genuinely doable now, is independently valuable, and is the correct way to talk about "predictions"
without overclaiming.

**First concrete move:** §4.5 — the parameterized finite-capacity/decoherence **bounds** paper.

**Exit criterion (the real one):** a completed Tier-2 substrate + Tier-3 emergence that *passes* the
microstate-counting, Page-curve, singularity, and cosmological tests, with at least one
parameter regime where finite-capacity effects are empirically distinguishable (or a rigorous proof
that they are Planck-suppressed). Passing §4.1–4.4 **is** the operational definition of "QIQT-H is a
theory of quantum gravity." Until then, the honest words remain: *single-world quantum foundations + a
conditional, classical, free-field emergent-gravity result, with a roadmap — not a solution — for the
rest.*
